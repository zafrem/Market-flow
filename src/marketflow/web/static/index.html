MarketFlow

See what is new and what people search for, then price it

MarketFlow is for food retail, fresh and processed. It spots new competitor products and fast-rising searches first, then compares wholesale, competitor and your own prices and proposes changes that protect your margin. A person approves every change; nothing is written to a sales channel.

  • Discover new competitor products and the most searched, fastest rising keywords
  • Trends trend radar, search-to-purchase gaps, category health
  • Prices and margin comparison matrix and recommendations that never go below the margin floor
